Site matching does not support feature:"curve" or reliably support feature:"merge"/"lane_drop", and may also fail globally for feature:"junction"; query unconstrained sites and construct unsupported features with portable props. Avoid disconnected map fragments. Parking-zone query results can advertise a long runway while binding ego to a short parking-access stub; simulate early and reject sites where ego stops near 10 m or route-relative actors disappear. Even unconstrained sites can bind the ego to an invalid or non-driving fragment despite a long advertised runway; if max ego speed is 0 in simulation while timed actors move, reject the site rather than tuning the interaction. An explicit ego speed interaction does not repair such immobilized bindings; switch sites or maps. Site changes can preserve actors/interactions; after a failed branch binding, remove the actor before re-querying. Signal phase keys and environmental rules are world-scoped and cannot be attached to ordinary actors.
