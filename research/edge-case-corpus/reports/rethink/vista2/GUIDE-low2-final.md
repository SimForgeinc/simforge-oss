Site matcher limitation: feature:"curve" is unmatchable. For bend scenarios, query feature:null with minLanes:2 and select a visibly curved candidate.
The feature:"junction" matcher may also return no feasible sites because features.f-junction.atM fails universally. If so, query feature:null and visually select a junction candidate.
The feature:"merge" and feature:"lane_drop" matchers may fail universally on their feature-atM clauses. For cut-in or closure-taper scenarios, query feature:null with minLanes:2 and construct the merge using adjacent-lane motion plus cones/channelizers.
Motion caveat: headingDeg changes actor orientation but may not reverse route progression. To force travel against the default lane route, use an explicit route motion with points ordered toward ego; do not rely on headingDeg alone.
Do not assume the sign of dLane identifies opposing traffic. Probe a visibly opposing lane with pixel placement and use the returned portable projection.
Cross-path limitation: solver-owned cross_path/nearMiss routes support pedestrians only, not vehicles. It may also mis-resolve pedestrian anchors on some sites, teleporting the route to ego’s spawn; if repeated rollout facts show a spawn artifact, replace it with an explicit lane-relative transverse route.
Animal actors appear excluded from finite TTC and required-deceleration calculation even when their crossing receives accept/critical; a separate traffic actor may be needed to satisfy C4.
Stopping schema: use motion {"kind":"speed","speedKph":"stop",...}; "stop" is not a standalone motion kind.
Ego placement offsets are absolute relative to the site’s reference pose, not cumulative across repeated placements. Actors anchored relative to ego may preserve relative spacing when ego is retuned.
For a parked vehicle that must count in clearance/TTC evaluation, do not set static:true. Use a non-static actor and explicitly apply a stop motion when persistent standstill is required.
For stationary-obstacle car-following scenarios, ego aggression can lower a conservative TTC buffer.
For delayed pedestrian emergence, add an at-t=0 speed stop interaction before triggering the later route, and explicitly restart walking speed when the route begins because the stop otherwise persists.
Do not infer forward direction from dsM sign, actor orientation, or apparent junction position. Use rollout keyframes and motion trails.
Pixel anchors projected onto broad junction polygons may be unroutable even when visually on pavement; if rollout speed remains zero despite restart commands, switch sites or use a verified lane-relative reference route.
Changing sites may preserve actors and interactions rather than resetting them; explicitly remove stale actors/interactions before rebuilding.
World-scoped rules such as env.frictionScale and signal:feature:<featureId>:<movement>.phase cannot be attached to a vehicle role. actor values world and __world__ are not valid reserved roles in the current API. Vehicle key lights.left is also unsupported; use supported behavioral rules when direct signal/light state cannot be authored.
