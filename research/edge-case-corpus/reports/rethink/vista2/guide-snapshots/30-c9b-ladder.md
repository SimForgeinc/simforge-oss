Site matching does not support feature:"curve" or reliably support feature:"merge"/"lane_drop", and may also fail globally for feature:"junction"; query unconstrained sites and construct unsupported features with portable props. Avoid disconnected map fragments. Site changes can preserve actors/interactions; after a failed branch binding, remove the actor before re-querying. Signal phase keys are world-scoped and cannot attach to actors. Use `read_pixels` to verify heading, lane center, overlap count, and projection; lane indices can be counterintuitive. Pixel anchors are emitted as portable lane-relative anchors, but ego-route-relative anchors are preferable. Vehicles with nonzero initial `tFrac` may remain stationary; spawn moving vehicles centered, then shift them using `lane_offset`. Geometrically valid branch vehicles may remain invisible or produce no interaction, so simulate early. Static scenery is excluded from TTC evaluation but can still cause physical collisions; keep evaluator-supporting traffic from driving into required props. Hold active vehicles with explicit speed commands; use numeric `speedKph:0` for a counted stop because string `"stop"` may be marked never-fired. Stop interactions may persist indefinitely, so use fixed-time follow-up commands rather than interaction-end triggers. `motion.gear` accepts only `forward` or `reverse`. `cross_path` is pedestrian-only and must be verified immediately. A portable pedestrian hesitate-cross pattern is ego 40 km/h; pedestrian at dsM 70/lateral +6 m with explicit perpendicular route, stop at t=1.5, restart 5 km/h at t=3.0. A portable pickup cut-in pattern is ego 50 km/h; centered pickup dsM 55 at 40 km/h; explicit 40 km/h at t=0; offset to tFrac -0.65 over 1 s at t=0.2; return center over 1.2 s at t=1.5; set speed to numeric 0 at t=3 with 6 m/s². On El Camino this produced about 2.0 m clearance, 2.46 s min-TTC, 1.96 m/s² required decel, critical acceptance, and no collision. For a static roadway-hazard brief, placing the prop transversely at dsM -25 on the ego lane keeps it literal and visible at placement while preventing forward actors from colliding with it; pair it with an active portable interaction because static props do not generate evaluator TTC. For a constructed lane-closure taper, add static channelizer drums on the closing-side edge at dsM/tFrac 80/-0.95, 88/-0.9, 96/-0.8, and 104/-0.7; pair them with the pickup cut-in pattern so traffic moves inward ahead of ego. A three-vehicle braking wave uses same-route leads at dsM 55/75/95, all 40 km/h, stopping at t=3.0/2.5/2.0 respectively at 6 m/s². A single-lane aggressive weave can use a hatchback dsM -32 at 78 km/h with offsets +0.9 at t=1.8, +0.1 at t=3.2, and +0.9 at t=4.1; the final escape is essential. For declared occlusion, a static covered car at dsM 24/lateral +4 can screen a pedestrian at dsM 30/lateral +5 until its crossing route reveals it. For an animal crossing, `animal.dog` may remain immobile despite fired speed and route commands. A working portable pattern uses ego 50 km/h and `animal.deer` at dsM 70, dLane 0, tFrac +1, initial 25 km/h, heading -90 degrees; route it at t=3.2 through dsM 70/tFrac +1, 0, and -1. On El Camino this yielded critical acceptance, min-TTC about 0.47 s after warmup, zero recorded collisions, and all triggers fired. Triggering at 3.5 was physically unavoidable; triggering at 2.6 was feasible but before an evaluator window beginning at 3.2.
