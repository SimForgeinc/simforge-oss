Site matching does not support feature:"curve" and may also fail globally for feature:"junction"; query unconstrained sites and identify such geometry visually when needed. Signal phase keys are world-scoped. They cannot attach to cars or static objects, and creating a normal role named `world` does not confer world scope. Uninstantiated owner names tested and rejected: `world`, `_world`, `env`, `environment`, `scenario`, `global`, `__world__`, `@world`, `$world`, feature IDs, and omitted actor. Avoid disconnected map fragments. Site changes can preserve actors/interactions. `cross_path` is pedestrian-only and can fire without producing movement or finite path-TTC; verify it immediately by simulation. Lane indices and longitudinal stationing can be counterintuitive: an opposing lane may project as dLane=0, while dLane=+1 may select a side branch. Use `read_pixels` to verify travel heading, overlap count, lane center, and portable projection; prefer unique lanes (`overlappingLanes: 1`). Geometrically valid opposing anchors can remain stationary despite placement speed or explicit speed motion, so verify actor motion early with `simulate`. Pixel-derived anchors can pass locally yet be wholly nonportable. Ego-route-relative lateral anchors avoid branch-prone lane indices but may still resolve to unroutable geometry. For centerline drift, tune a moderate lane offset down from a known collision-producing value. Vehicles marked `static:true` become scenery props and may be excluded from interaction/TTC evaluation; represent parked hazards as non-static vehicle actors. A non-static vehicle placed at 0 km/h can unexpectedly accelerate along its route; hold it with motion `{kind:"speed", speedKph:"stop", trigger:{at:0}}` (there is no standalone `kind:"stop"`). Speed-stop commands may not persist for a full 16 s rollout; refresh the command later if the actor resumes. `motion.gear` accepts only `forward` or `reverse`, not `park`. A portable post-warmup dilemma pattern is ego at 50 km/h with a same-route lead at dsM=55 and 40 km/h, commanded to stop at t=3 with rate 6 m/s²; locally this yielded minTTC 2.40 s, required decel 2.12 m/s², 2.01 m clearance, and no collision. For a visible loss-of-traction lead event on a straight junction approach, ego and lead both at 50 km/h, lead at dsM=52, lane_offset tFrac=+0.75 for 1.5 s at t=2, and simultaneous stop at 6 m/s² can yield a portable-looking critical stop; on Belmont 09abdf50 it produced minTTC 2.95 s, required decel 2.25 m/s², 2.20 m clearance, and no collision. Branching sites can distort this pattern by reducing warmup speeds or routing the lead away, so validate on a straight connected corridor.
